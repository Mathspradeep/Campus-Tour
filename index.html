Campus Tour — Rotation Schedule

Five teams move through all 31 venues together, one time slot at a time. Every team follows the same route, offset from the others — so no two teams are ever standing in the same room.

Each team's starting point is spaced 6 venues apart (venues 1, 7, 13, 19, 25) and every team simply advances to the next venue in sequence each slot, wrapping from 31 back to 1. Because the five starting offsets are all different, the five teams can never land on the same venue in the same slot — by construction, not by checking. After 31 slots, every team has visited every venue exactly once.
